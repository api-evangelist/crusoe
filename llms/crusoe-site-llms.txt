# Crusoe Cloud Documentation


> Crusoe is the AI factory company — a vertically integrated AI infrastructure provider that designs, builds, and operates energy-first AI infrastructure. Crusoe Cloud is the company's AI cloud platform, offering managed inference, GPU compute, orchestration, and storage services purpose-built for large-scale AI workloads. Crusoe controls its entire stack: power sourcing, manufacturing, data center construction, cloud infrastructure, and managed AI services. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Denver, CO. Raised over $1.375 billion at a valuation above $10 billion. Recognized as one of Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies of 2026. NVIDIA Preferred Partner; collaborates with AMD.

## Key Differentiators


- Vertically integrated: Controls energy, manufacturing, data centers, cloud platform, and managed AI services end-to-end
- Purpose-built for AI: All infrastructure designed from day one for AI workloads, not retrofitted
- Deployment velocity: Power secured before breaking ground; Spark units deploy in months; GPU clusters provision in minutes
- Self-healing infrastructure: AutoClusters automatically detects, isolates, repairs, and restarts hardware faults (including XID79 GPU errors) without human intervention
- Expert partnership: Engineers embed directly with customers to co-architect clusters; not a ticket queue
- Open architecture, zero lock-in: No proprietary walled gardens; no egress fees; open standards throughout
- MemoryAlloy™: Up to 9.9x faster TTFT and 5x higher throughput vs. vLLM out of the box
- 100% renewable energy coverage: All electricity matched with renewable energy via direct sourcing, vPPAs, or EACs

## Available Models

Models available via Crusoe Managed Inference, served on Crusoe's proprietary inference engine with MemoryAlloy™ technology. All models are accessible via an OpenAI-compatible API endpoint at api.inference.crusoecloud.com and via the Intelligence Foundry chat interface. Crusoe also supports Bring Your Own Model (BYOM) for custom or fine-tuned models. Pricing is listed on the Crusoe Cloud pricing page.
