Security · Access Management · Security Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) now support GCP Cloud KMS in addition to AWS KMS. Register a Cloud KMS key through workload identity federation to encrypt and decrypt your data with a key you own in your GCP project. See Customer-Managed Keys .
