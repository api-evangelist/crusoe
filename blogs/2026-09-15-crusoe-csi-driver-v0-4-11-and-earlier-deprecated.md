Infrastructure Cloud · Managed Kubernetes · Service update · ⚠ Breaking Crusoe CSI driver versions earlier than v0.4.12 are deprecated and will become unsupported after November 8, 2026. Clusters running in a private network without outbound internet access must upgrade before then. See Upgrade the Crusoe CSI driver .
